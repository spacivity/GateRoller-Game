# Gate Roller Atlantis

**Roll fast. Turn sharp. Find Atlantis.**

Gate Roller Atlantis is a neon arcade adventure that follows four daring
skaters on their search for the legendary lost city. Players must survive 130
handcrafted arenas, master an ever-growing collection of hazards, uncover
twelve ancient clues, and open the path to the Heart of Atlantis.

Developed and published by **Spacivity Ltd trading as Spacivity Games**.

## The Adventure

Ancient ruins hide a secret beneath their glowing stone walls. Guide Roller
Ball, Daizy, Chaz, and Leroy through a sprawling campaign filled with forgotten
technology, hostile guardians, lost artifacts, and mysterious gateways.

Every completed chapter brings the skaters closer to Atlantis. After uncovering
all twelve clues, players must solve an ancient ritual to reveal ten final
sunken-city levels and reach the Heart of Atlantis.

## How It Plays

The skater moves continuously, leaving a luminous trail behind. Turn through
each arena without crashing into walls, enemies, traps, the arena edge, or your
own trail. Survive until the gate activates, then reach the exit before the
retracting trail catches up with you.

Simple swipe and directional controls make the game easy to learn, while faster
difficulties, layered hazards, and increasingly complex arenas reward timing,
planning, and quick reactions.

## Key Features

- **130 handcrafted levels** spanning ancient ruins and the sunken city of
  Atlantis
- **Four unlockable skaters**, each with a distinctive appearance, neon trail,
  and speed profile
- **An evolving arcade campaign** featuring portals, bumpers, lasers, hostile

- **Powerful boosters** including extra lives, speed effects, shields, weapons,

- **Artifact hunts and hidden treasures** inside large scrolling arenas
- **An Atlantis mystery** built around twelve clues and an ancient ritual
- **Four difficulty settings:** Slow, Normal, Fast, and Insane
- **Flexible mobile controls** with swipe gestures and an on-screen directional

- **Portrait and landscape support** for iPhone and iPad
- **Game Center integration** with achievements and a high-score leaderboard
- **Persistent progress** with checkpoints, Level Select, and saved character
  unlocks
- **A replayable interactive tutorial** introducing movement, boosters, and

- **Arcade presentation** with animated neon trails, pixel-art effects, an
  original soundtrack, sound effects, and optional haptic feedback

## Hazards and Abilities

No two chapters play quite the same. Early arenas teach precise movement and
trail management before introducing teleport portals, pinball bumpers, sweeping
lasers, projectile-firing enemies, wraparound corridors, collectible vehicles,
spider webs, reflective walls, and the deadly whirlpools of Atlantis.

Boosters can change the flow of a run by slowing or accelerating movement,
granting an extra life, shielding the skater from lasers, arming powerful plasma
shots, smashing enemies, or allowing safe passage through the neon trail.

## Designed for Mobile

Gate Roller Atlantis adapts its interface and playfield for portrait and
landscape orientations on iPhone and iPad. Players can steer with directional
swipes or the on-screen pad, pause at any time, adjust the game speed, and
independently control music, sound effects, and haptic feedback.

## Play for Free

Levels 1 through 19 are free to play. The complete 130-level adventure can be
unlocked with one non-consumable in-app purchase. Purchase restoration is
supported through the App Store.
