# Privacy Policy for Gate Roller Atlantis

**Effective date: September 01, 2026**

Gate Roller Atlantis is provided by Spacivity Ltd trading as Spacivity Games
("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is
handled when you use the Gate Roller Atlantis mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. The App does not use
advertising services, analytics services, or tracking technologies, and we do
not sell or share personal information for advertising.

The App stores gameplay information locally on your device, including campaign
progress, scores, unlocked levels and characters, selected difficulty, audio
and haptic preferences, collected treasures, and full-game unlock status. We do
not receive this locally stored information.

## Apple Services

The App uses services provided by Apple:

- **Game Center:** If you use Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center
  account information, leaderboard scores, and achievements. Your use of Game
  Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your Apple account settings.
- **In-App Purchases:** Purchases and purchase restoration are processed by
  Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card or banking
  details. Apple provides the App with the transaction status needed to unlock
  purchased content.

Apple's handling of information is governed by the
[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Support Communications

If you contact us for support, we receive the email address you use and any
information you include in your message. We use that information only to reply
to your request, troubleshoot the issue, and maintain necessary support
records. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive
information.

We retain support correspondence only for as long as reasonably necessary to
resolve requests, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations. You
may ask us to delete your support correspondence by contacting us at the email
address below, subject to any records we must retain by law.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you
believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support
request, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

## Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards for information sent to us in support
communications. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely
secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## Your Choices

You can manage Game Center through your device's Apple account settings. You
can remove locally stored gameplay data by deleting the App. A previously
purchased full-game unlock can be restored through the App using the same Apple
account, subject to Apple's services and policies.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or applicable
requirements. Any revised policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
