# Gate Roller Atlantis Support

Gate Roller Atlantis is developed and published by Spacivity Ltd trading as
Spacivity Games.

Need help with Gate Roller Atlantis? Email
[support+gaterolleratlantis@spacivity.com](mailto:support+gaterolleratlantis@spacivity.com).

To help us investigate quickly, include your device model, iOS or iPadOS
version, App version, and a short description of what happened. If possible,
also include the level number, the steps that reproduce the issue, and a
screenshot. Please do not send passwords or payment information.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I control my skater?

Use the on-screen directional pad or swipe left, right, up, or down. You can
make a perpendicular turn, but you cannot reverse directly into your trail.
A short tap turns clockwise. The App supports both portrait and landscape play
on iPhone and iPad.

### How do I pause or resume?

Tap the Menu button to pause and open the in-game menu. Tap or swipe the play
area while paused to resume.

### How is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on your device whenever you cross a gate. The App records
your next level, score, lives, unlocks, selected skater, difficulty, and
preferences. Cleared levels remain available through Level Select when you
start a new run.

Deleting the App may remove locally saved campaign progress. Game Center data
and App Store purchases are managed separately by Apple.

### How do I unlock the full game?

Levels 1 through 19 are free. Entering level 20 presents the option to unlock
the complete game with a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open the full-game unlock screen and select **Restore Purchase**. Make sure the
device is signed in to the Apple account originally used for the purchase and
has an internet connection. You will not be charged again for restoring the
same non-consumable purchase.

If restoration still fails, restart the App and try again. For unresolved
billing or purchase-history issues, contact
[Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/billing).

### Why are my Game Center scores or achievements not appearing?

Confirm that you are signed in to Game Center in your device settings and have
an internet connection. Then reopen the App. Scores and achievements may take
a short time to appear in Apple's Game Center interface.

### Can I replay the tutorial or an earlier level?

Yes. The main menu includes a replayable training level and Level Select for
levels you have unlocked.

### Can I change the game speed?

Yes. From the main menu, choose Slow, Normal, Fast, or Insane. The selected
difficulty is saved for future sessions.

### How do I change music, sound effects, or haptic feedback?

Use the controls in the main menu to toggle music, sound effects, and haptic
feedback. Your choices are saved on your device.
