# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to GATEROLLER.

## Summary

GATEROLLER is designed as an offline game. The app does not create user accounts, does not collect personal information, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not sell or share personal data.

## Information Stored on Your Device

GATEROLLER stores a small amount of gameplay and preference data locally on your device, including:

- high score and last score
- saved level progress
- completed levels and bonus rounds
- best-name entry, if you choose to enter one
- tutorial, music, and sound-effect preferences

This information is stored locally using the device's app storage. It is used only to restore your game progress and preferences.

## Information We Do Not Collect

GATEROLLER does not collect:

- name, email address, phone number, or account credentials
- precise location
- contacts
- photos, camera, or microphone data
- payment information
- advertising identifiers
- analytics or tracking data

## Network, Analytics, and Advertising

GATEROLLER does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or in-app tracking in the app source.

Platform services, such as the Apple App Store, Google Play, Expo, or the operating system, may process technical information under their own privacy policies. That processing is separate from GATEROLLER's in-app behavior.

## Permissions

GATEROLLER uses audio and haptic features for gameplay feedback. The app does not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.

On Android, the app declares audio settings access so it can manage game audio behavior.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay and preference data remains on your device until you delete the app, clear the app's storage, or reset data through any available in-app controls.

Because GATEROLLER does not transmit gameplay data to a server, we cannot remotely access, export, or delete local data on your behalf.

## Children's Privacy

GATEROLLER does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not include accounts, ads, analytics, or social features.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's data practices change. Any update should revise the "Last updated" date above.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app publisher through the app store listing or replace this line with the official support email before publication.
