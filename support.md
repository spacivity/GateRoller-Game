# Support

For GATEROLLER support, email:

support@spacivity.com

## What to Include

When contacting support, include:

- your device model
- your operating system version
- the app version, if available
- a short description of the issue
- the steps that caused the issue, if you can reproduce it

Please do not send sensitive personal information.

## Common Troubleshooting

If the app is not behaving as expected:

- close and reopen the app
- restart your device
- check that you are using the latest app version
- confirm your device audio and silent-mode settings if sound is missing

## Saved Progress

GATEROLLER stores progress and preferences locally on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its app storage may remove saved progress, high scores, and audio preferences.

Because progress is stored locally, support cannot restore deleted local progress remotely.
