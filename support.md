# GATEROLLER Support

Last updated: July 31, 2026

For gameplay, purchase, or technical support, email
[support@spacivity.com](mailto:support@spacivity.com).

## What to Include

Include the following when reporting a problem:

- device model and operating-system version
- GATEROLLER app version, if available
- whether the app came from Apple App Store or Google Play
- level number and whether the device was in portrait or landscape
- input method used: tap, swipe, touchscreen controls, or hardware keyboard
- a short description and reproducible steps
- a screenshot or short screen recording when it helps explain the issue

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, store-account credentials, full
purchase receipts, or other sensitive personal information.

## Controls and Gameplay

- Swipe in a direction to launch the ball.
- Tap while moving to turn clockwise, or swipe in a valid direction to steer.
- A direct reverse is blocked unless the reverse-choice booster is active.
- On shooting levels, an accepted turn fires immediately. Rapid repetitive
  tapping may be rate-limited to keep gameplay responsive.
- Supported installed mobile builds can also use external arrow keys. Keyboard
  capture is disabled while menus, tutorials, or text entry are open.
- The app pauses when it leaves the foreground. Select **RESUME** after
  returning if the run remains paused.

## Common Troubleshooting

If the app is not behaving as expected:

1. Close and reopen GATEROLLER.
2. Confirm that the latest available app version is installed.
3. Restart the device.
4. If movement still stutters, note the level, orientation, input method, and
   what was visible on screen when it happened, then contact support.

If controls appear unresponsive, make sure a menu, tutorial, purchase screen,
or name-entry prompt is not covering the game. A reverse-direction swipe is
normally rejected by the game rules.

## Audio and Haptics

Music and sound effects can be enabled or disabled separately from the in-game
menu. If sound is missing, confirm both in-game settings and the device volume
or silent-mode settings. Haptic feedback depends on device support and device
settings.

## Saved Progress

GATEROLLER stores checkpoints, completed levels, scores, lives, the optional
score-display name, purchase entitlement, and preferences locally in the app's
device storage.

Deleting the app or clearing its storage may remove this data. An
operating-system backup may preserve or transfer local data, but availability
depends on the Apple or Google backup settings. Support cannot remotely access
or restore deleted local progress because gameplay data is not sent to a
GATEROLLER-operated server.

Use **CHECKPOINT** or **CONTINUE** from the menu to resume the saved run. **NEW
RUN** starts again from the training level.

## Full Game Purchase

The Full Game is a one-time, non-consumable purchase that unlocks level 15 and
all later stages in the current 91-stage build.

If a previous purchase is not recognized:

1. Confirm that the device is signed in to the Apple or Google account that
   originally completed the purchase.
2. Check the internet connection and wait for the purchase screen to show that
   the store is ready.
3. Select **RESTORE PURCHASE** on the Full Game screen.
4. Close and reopen the app, then try restore once more.
5. Contact support with the platform, app version, and exact on-screen message
   if restoration still fails.

A pending purchase unlocks only after the storefront confirms completion.
Apple App Store and Google Play purchases are separate and do not transfer
between store accounts or platforms. Apple or Google manages charges, refunds,
payment methods, and purchase-history records.

## Privacy

Do not include sensitive information in a support request. For details about
local data, storefront processing, permissions, backups, and deletion, read
the [GATEROLLER Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).
